A full single-variable Calculus course.

We open with the precalculus the subject rests on — trigonometry and the unit circle, sets, relations, functions, logarithms and exponentials — and then take up the main ideas of calculus: limits and rates of change, the derivative and the rules of differentiation, applications of the derivative, and finally integration and both Fundamental Theorems of Calculus.

This is an earlier offering; see Calculus 1 (2024) for the most recent version.
