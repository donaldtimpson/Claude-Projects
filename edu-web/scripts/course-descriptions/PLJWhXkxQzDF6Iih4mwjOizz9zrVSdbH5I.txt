A calculus-based introductory Physics course (Physics 1), covering Newtonian and classical mechanics.

We work through kinematics in one and several dimensions, Newton's laws and their applications, work and energy, momentum and collisions, rotational motion and angular momentum, and gravitation.

See University Physics I (2025) for the most recent mechanics course.
