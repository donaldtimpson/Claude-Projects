A first course in General (Point-Set) Topology.

Beginning from set theory and a review of relations and functions, we develop topological spaces, open and closed sets and bases, continuity and homeomorphism, and the three C's of topology — continuity, connectedness, and compactness — along with the separation axioms.

This is an earlier offering; see General Topology (Point Set Topology) for the most recent version.
