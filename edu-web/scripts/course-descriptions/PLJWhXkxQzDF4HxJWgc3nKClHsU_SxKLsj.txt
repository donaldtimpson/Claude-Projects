An introductory Discrete Mathematics course — the foundations a proof-based mathematical education is built on.

We cover logic and the methods of proof, sets and functions, sequences, induction and recursion, counting and combinatorics, relations, and an introduction to graphs and trees.

This is an earlier offering; see Discrete Math (2025) for the most recent version.
