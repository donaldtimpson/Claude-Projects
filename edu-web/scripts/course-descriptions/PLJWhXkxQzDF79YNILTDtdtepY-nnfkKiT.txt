This is intended as an undergraduate-level, first-semester, General Topology course. We begin with Set Theory, and work our way through the three Cs of Topology (Continuity, Connectedness, and Compactness).

The course is a continuation of my Logic playlist, and so it starts with Set Theory and a review of Relations and Functions, then gets into Topology proper starting on Lecture 5.
