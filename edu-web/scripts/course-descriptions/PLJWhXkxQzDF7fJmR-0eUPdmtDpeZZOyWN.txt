A History of Ancient Western Philosophy course.

After a short review of logic and some historical context, we begin with the Presocratics — Thales, Anaximander, Anaximenes, Xenophanes, Heraclitus, Pythagoras — and follow the tradition forward through the classical period.

This is an earlier offering; see History of Philosophy (2024) for the most recent version.
