A college-level Discrete Mathematics course.

Starting from logic and careful reasoning, we work through methods of proof, sets and functions, sequences and induction, counting and combinatorics, relations, and graph theory — the toolkit for the rest of a mathematics education.

This is an earlier offering; see Discrete Math (2025) for the most recent version.
