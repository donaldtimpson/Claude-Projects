A college-level Discrete Mathematics course.

We begin with careful reasoning — definitions, theorems, proof, and counterexample — and build through Boolean algebra, lists, sets and quantifiers, combinatorics and the binomial theorem, relations and functions, permutations, and induction, before turning to probability and number theory. It closes with a review of trigonometry.

This is an earlier offering; see Discrete Math (2025) for the most recent version.
