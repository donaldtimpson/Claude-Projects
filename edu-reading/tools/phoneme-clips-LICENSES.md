# Letter-sound clips (ios/Resources/Sounds/ph_*.m4a)

Source: FreeReading, "Most common letter sounds (audio clips)"
https://www.freereading.net/wiki/Most_common_letter_sounds_(audio_clips).html
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 (FreeReading content licence;
see https://www.freereading.net/wiki/About_Free-Reading.html and
https://wiki.creativecommons.org/wiki/Free-reading.net).

Each original clip is the sound followed by an example word ("/k/ ... kit"). The
sound alone was cut at the silence before the word, faded (8 ms in, 30 ms out),
and levelled to the same loudness (loudest 200 ms window at -16 dBFS, peaks
<= -1 dBFS). Every cut was verified by transcribing the remainder: each came back
as exactly the example word. These adapted clips are licensed CC BY-SA 3.0.

Mapping: c and k share ph_k (from "k ... kit"); q -> ph_kw ("quit"); x -> ph_ks ("fox").

Not covered (no open human recording found): sh, ch, th (both), ng, long vowels,
vowel teams, r-controlled vowels, ou/oi/aw. The app says those words whole rather
than sounding them out, and never plays a synthesised phoneme.
